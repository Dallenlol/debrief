# Code of Conduct

Be kind, be direct, assume good intent. Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks are not welcome anywhere in this project. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues or contributors that violate this, and will explain why.

Report problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub's private reporting (Security tab > Report a vulnerability, and say it is a conduct report), or open an issue that only asks a maintainer to contact you, without details.
